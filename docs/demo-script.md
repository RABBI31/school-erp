# Demo Script (about 20 minutes)

Before: `php artisan migrate:fresh --seed`, open `http://demo.localhost:8000`, logged in as admin, Bangla on. Phone charged, backup video on the laptop, no other tabs open.

## 1. Listen first (5–10 min)
Ask the discovery questions before opening the laptop:
- How do you manage results, fees and attendance now?
- What takes the most time or causes the most parent complaints?
- Students, shifts, versions (Bangla/English medium)?
- Have you used school software before? What went wrong?
- Who would use it every day?
- Can I take a copy of your mark sheet, fee receipt and student list?

Then show the parts that match what they just told you first.

## 2. Dashboard (1 min)
"This is what you see every morning": today's attendance, this month's collection, total dues.

## 3. Results (5 min) — the strongest part
1. Exams → Half Yearly → Class 9 → Section A → Mathematics mark entry. Enter a few marks quickly with the keyboard.
2. Show that a teacher can only see their own subjects.
3. Open a published result → one student's mark sheet PDF in Bangla.
4. Tabulation sheet for the whole section with merit positions.
5. Result summary: pass rate and subject-wise fails.
Say: "Result sheets that take your teachers days are ready in minutes."

## 4. Fees (4 min)
1. Collect fee → search a student → dues by month → take a partial payment → print receipt.
2. Dues list for Class 8 → "Send due SMS".
3. Today's collection report by collector.
Say: "You always know exactly who owes what, and every taka has a receipt."

## 5. Attendance (2 min)
1. On your phone: take attendance for a section, mark two absent, save.
2. Show the absent SMS in the SMS log.
Say: "Parents know the same morning."

## 6. Students & setup (2 min)
Admission of a new student with a sibling already enrolled (guardian reused). Show import from Excel.

## 7. Close (3 min)
- "This is the product. Results and fees are working now; we'll fit everything to your school's format during the pilot."
- Offer: pilot for 2–3 months, you set up their data and train staff; in return feedback and a reference.
- Next step: "Give me your mark sheet and receipt format; I'll show you your own school in the system next week."

## If something breaks
Don't debug live. Say "let me show you this part on the recording" and play the backup video.
